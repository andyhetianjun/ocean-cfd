Ocean CFD work from a co-op term at the Bedford Institute of Oceanography (Fisheries and Oceans Canada). Two related strands:

Wave-flume simulations in OpenFOAM / waves2Foam, regular waves, a cylinder, and combined wave + current cases.
Passive tracer transport -> taking archived LES velocity from a monopile-in-current simulation and using it to advect a passive tracer, to see how the structure's wake redistributes material in the water column.
